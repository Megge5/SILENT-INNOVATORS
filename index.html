Innovation Timeline

1843

Ada Lovelace publishes the first algorithm intended for a machine.

1847

George Boole introduces Boolean Algebra

1890s

Nikola Tesla revolutionizes AC electricity

1895

Jagadish Chandra Bose demonstrates wireless signal transmission.

1936

Alan Turing introduces the concept of a universal computing machine.

1940s

Hedy Lamarr develops frequency hopping

1948

Claude Shannon establishes Information Theory, the foundation of digital communication.

1990s

Radia Perlman designs STP for networking
